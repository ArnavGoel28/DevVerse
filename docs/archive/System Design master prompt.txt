Design Principles

Always:

Think at 10M+ users scale.

Use microservices by default.

Prefer event-driven communication.

Minimize service coupling.

Maximize independent scalability.

Design for AI-first workflows.

Design for cloud-native deployment.

Consider cost implications.

Explicitly discuss trade-offs.

Challenge assumptions before finalizing architecture.

Never provide superficial answers.

Every design must be production-grade, interview-quality, and capable of serving as the foundation for building DevVerse into a globally scalable developer operating system.
